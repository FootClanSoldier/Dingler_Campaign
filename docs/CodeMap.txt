DINGLER CAMPAIGN CODE MAP
========================

Dingler.Game/Campaign/
    Campaign domain/state and the bridge from campaign encounters into Dingler
    battle sessions. Keep this separate from battle simulation itself.

Dingler.Game/Arena/
    Frost Ring Arena domain/battle state. Working functionality; campaign work
    must not repurpose ArenaBattleService as campaign state storage.

Dingler.Game/Games/
    Shared game/session engine integration around original HEX rules classes.

Dingler.Game.Protocol/
    Wire message decoding/encoding and custom protocol request wrappers.

Dingler.Server/
    Connection/session context and response delivery infrastructure.

Campaign bootstrap and state
----------------------------
AZ1 authored map topology:
    CampaignAz1MapCatalog and embedded Campaign/Definitions/Az1Map.json store the
    original-client-derived 85 authored logical locations, 79 physical Unity
    objects and 83 undirected paths, with inferred variant bindings explicitly
    distinguished. The physical junction Fork001 and unmapped logical state
    variants are not projected as actionable AREA locations. The 76 verified
    direct locations can be projected hidden/disabled, without awarding or
    enabling their unknown actions. See docs/Research/HEX_AZ1_Original_Map_Catalog_Research.md.
    CampaignStateFactory.EnsureAz1MapState only adds missing direct entries,
    preserving save fields and existing node flags. SyncAz1OpeningExitLocks
    computes complete known map locks; only five implemented opening edges
    may unlock when their current authored prerequisites are satisfied.
    CampaignService validates path objects against the catalog, explicitly
    gates all actual movement by implemented opening locations and map locks,
    never moves on batched visit_path, and traces AZ1 visibility/lock state.
    For movement to an authored destination, validates a connected route over
    unlocked opening edges with previously visited intermediate nodes; revisiting
    an earlier node may not emit visit_node in the original client, so an
    adjacent-only last-node test is insufficient.
    Winning the Dunnwood encounter makes both Node004 and Node007 travelable
    when their original content templates resolve; capturing Dire Toad advances
    Tamed independently. Node004 has its original intro/reference-card chooser;
    Node007 has verified first/repeat Milosh conversations. Closing Milosh's
    conversation (regardless of optional Fortune purchase) unlocks Node009
    and its authored Cockatwice prelude/encounter when templates resolve;
    later outgoing AZ1 paths remain locked. Paid Fortune transactions use
    an isolated 1000-Gold bootstrap display for testing; no real currency
    is charged and no Fortune is awarded.

Original client AREA fog-of-war research:
    docs/Research/HEX_Area_Fog_Of_War_Assembly_Research.md verifies the
    Location.Visible -> UIDungeonMapNode.IsVisible -> FOW animator path,
    FOWPostEffect image compositing and prefab dependencies. Live AZ1 terrain
    FOW is unconfirmed and not a current development requirement; preserve
    visibility and path-lock gameplay fields, but avoid shader/FOW changes.

    Existing AREA is reused without regenerating its GameplayState or CampID.
    Ensures the two AZ1 opening QUEST children idempotently and commits both
    their creation and the validated AREA conversation completion in one
    champion aggregate, retaining current AREA identity.
    On loading a completed report from an earlier saved aggregate, repairs its
    missing travel-NPC state once and atomically saves the same champion file.
    Finished quests are excluded from getactive while getcampstate remains addressable.

Campaign service / ServiceCampaign protocol
-------------------------------------------
Dingler.Game/Campaign/CampaignService.cs
    Main CampSysGeneral campaign state machine. Handles type-aware campaign queries,
    starter conversation progression, trainer-to-encounter/gameendnotify flow, the
    quest-giver QUEST/DUNGEON handoff, Crayburn conversation-node progression,
    castle encounter result handoffs and final Crayburn -> QUEST Step2 -> retained
    panorama return state. Dungeon wins/losses become race-authored result conversations;
    success completion advances the castle (or completes it at PenworthTower) and failure
    completion restores the encounter for retry. Race-specific quest-giver report
    conv_done atomically finishes Step2/QUEST and the returned PANORAMA NPC state,
    with non-transitioning QUEST and PANORAMA cmpupdates. The completed report
    also exposes the race intro NPC's distinct Feralroot travel conversation;
    closing it never restarts the tutorial. An explicit choice_go during the
    completed quest travel conversation atomically selects one AZ1 AREA and
    queues its transition; choice_stay leaves PANORAMA unchanged. AREA queries
    and limited Node001/Node002/Node003 travel use distinct persisted state.
    Its path-name validation uses the full embedded original prefab graph, but
    only implemented opening nodes may be entered. Batched visit_path does not
    move the champion; unknown/locked path events are ignored. Debug/info
    snapshots include visible/enabled nodes and visited/locked path counts.
    For AREA StartLoc, ALoc identifies the authored VisLoc Data.name while
    LastNode and visited_nodes identify the original map-node ID. AREA Node002
    conv_done accepts both the current location name and the legacy Node002 ID;
    successful authored welcome completion reveals Dunnwood via a non-transition
    cmpupdate. Repeated identical StartLoc requests do not rewrite state, and
    the first effective StartLoc logs its active identity and conversation.
    Completion of the verified AZ1 welcome spawns Tamed and the faction's
    authored Find quest through campspawn. Dunnwood first presents its original
    Feralroot Bear prelude, then exposes the Wild Cub encounter through the
    existing Dingler/original HEX battle flow. Authoritative game-end updates
    Dunnwood and reveals its two authored neighboring nodes on victory, but
    opens both authored exits on first battle win, using independent native
    scene/conversation checks for the destination actions. Later losses do not
    relock the exits, and an uncaptured victory remains retryable through Explore.
    Node007's original Milosh first/repeat dialogue is available, but its paid
    gaal_camp_accept event is accepted only within the active verified Milosh
    first/repeat dialogue. Under an explicit temporary development exception,
    the choice lets the original dialogue continue without a real debit or
    Fortune grant. conv_done clears pending choice state, restores the repeat dialogue,
    and reveals authored Node009 independent of a Fortune purchase. Node009
    prelude conv_done prepares its original encounter; the usual Dingler/HEX
    battle session must win with native Chickatwice capture AND current QUEST
    Tame2 to advance Tame3 and complete Node009. Loss or a win without matching
    capture retains a retryable encounter, without unlocking
    unimplemented onward paths or fabricating encounter rewards. Separately, Node004's introduction conv_done retains ALoc
    and returns an original-client ShroomHaus selector with three verified
    reference-template GUIDs. shroom_choice validates the active offer,
    persists one choice, returns an Applied card reward and prevents replay.
    Loss preserves retryability. Genuine AREA Empty locations consume
    empty_done once; stale Dunnwood empty_done cannot complete the encounter.
    Later AZ1 encounters beyond Node009, conditional taming outcomes, rewards
    and map nodes remain separate. Battle simulation stays elsewhere.

Campaign battle bridge
----------------------
AZ1 Dunnwood capture-gated progression:
    CampaignService.ApplyBattleResult accepts native captured-card template GUIDs
    alongside the win/loss result. It completes Dunnwood and QUEST az01_tamed's
    matching Tame1 objective only after a win plus a native Dire Toad capture
    (CardTemplate c0bb008e-17c5-4e73-baf9-5cbd8f3eb6dc from live HEX logs).
    Uncaptured wins retain an enabled, retryable encounter; a separate
    bear_defeated receipt latches both Node003 exits after any victory, and is
    not cleared by a later loss. AZ1 hydration repairs qualifying saved states
    without inferring victories from merely visible nodes. AREA and QUEST are
    persisted in the same champion file
    with one CampaignRunStore.Save; the client receives gameendnotify and a
    separate QUEST cmpupdate (Reason=quest_progress, Transition=false).
    Further Tamed encounters/rewards are not implemented by this change.

Dingler.Game/Campaign/CampaignBattleService.cs
    Owns campaign StartEncounter reservations, JoinSession validation and active
    campaign battle tracking. Validates camp_<CampaignID>, ActiveEncounterGuid,
    champion and selected PvE deck. Finished results are forwarded to CampaignService
    for progression. At the live GameEnded callback, reads original HEX
    TamedCards receipts and passes resolved original template GUIDs with win/loss
    to CampaignService. Duplicate end callbacks are ignored for the same battle.
    It must remain separate from ArenaBattleService.

    A failed original InitEncounter produces a read-only HEX SceneData
    comparison via CampaignEncounterDiagnostics before JoinSession refusal.

Dingler.Game/Handlers/Request/Arena/ArenaBattleRequestHandlers.cs
    Shared LoadBalancer/session protocol surface. Routes StartEncounter,
    JoinSession and ReadyToStartGame between Frost Ring and Campaign based on
    flags/session ownership while preserving the Arena path.

    ServiceCampaign sendevent(start)
      -> unsolicited gamestarted
      -> StartEncounter
      -> CampaignBattleService reservation
      -> JoinSession
      -> CampaignBattleService creates campaign game
      -> original PvE initializer loads CampaignTest + Gareth Kay
      -> ReadyToStartGame
      -> first original-HEX session event batch reaches the authenticated client
      -> playable training battle
      -> Gareth/ServiceAI eliminated by ChampionDefeated
      -> authoritative result callback reports won=true
      -> match is removed from the game registry

Current verified post-battle campaign route:
    finished campaign battle
      -> CampaignService applies training win/loss to persisted GameplayState
      -> final game-session event batch has drained
      -> unsolicited gameendnotify carries the updated GameState
      -> authored trainer success conversation becomes active
      -> trainer conv_done consumes TrainingVictoryPending
      -> trainer is hidden/completed/disabled
      -> race-specific quest giver becomes active
      -> original client sends quest-giver conv_done.

Game engine / original HEX reuse
--------------------------------
Dingler.Game/Games/GameManager.cs
    Creates and registers HexGameWrapper/HexRulesEngine games. Campaign uses a
    dedicated CreateCampaignGame path rather than forcing campaign through the
    Frost Ring game-creation path. The campaign human seat is explicitly bound
    to the authenticated account name used by SessionManager. Campaign progression
    is invoked only after HexGameWrapper.RunGameAsync returns, so the final battle
    session events flush before ServiceCampaign game-end notification.
    At the campaign-only CreateCampaignGame boundary, initializes
    SessionStateEncounterData.AbilitiesToRun when absent on the client-returned
    StartEncounter payload. Original HEX InitEncounter appends authored scene
    modifier abilities to this list before loading players/decks; existing
    client-supplied ability IDs must be preserved. Does not affect Arena.


Dingler.Game/Games/HexGameWrapper.cs
    Queues and delivers original-HEX game events to connected clients. Supports
    an explicit player-UID -> authenticated username binding because a campaign
    champion's display name is not necessarily the login/account name. Existing
    champion-name lookup remains as fallback for Arena/tournament behavior. The
    same binding is used to resolve the player during reconnect.

Dingler.Game/Games/HexRulesEngine.Campaign.cs
    Campaign-specific seam into original HEX PvE initialization. Supplies the
    persisted campaign champion/deck through HasDeckAndChampInfo-compatible data
    and lets original IsEncounter|IsPvE behavior resolve the encounter scene/AI.
    Its campaign-only end-of-game reader inspects the human champion's native
    PermanentData/TamedCards capture receipts, resolves their SessionCardIds via
    original SessionCardCache, logs details and returns only verified original
    template GUIDs. It does not itself advance quests or grant captured cards.

Dingler.Game/Games/HexRulesEngine.Arena.cs
    Frost Ring-specific engine bootstrap. Do not change for campaign merely to
    share code unless a later verified need proves the behavior is generic.

Dingler.Game/Arena/Ai/ArenaAiSeat.cs
    Existing local original-HEX AI seat/pump used by Frost Ring and currently
    reused as the seat adapter for campaign AI.

Dingler.Game/Handlers/Request/Game/ReadyForGameSetupRequestHandler.cs
    Game setup protocol handler. Keep diagnostics/routing consistent with the

Important campaign invariants
-----------------------------
- Campaign state and battle simulation are separate responsibilities.
- Active encounter identity is persisted as ActiveEncounterGuid before battle.
- Session names use camp_<CampaignID> for campaign battles.
- Campaign battle flags are IsEncounter | IsPvE, not IsPvEArena.
- Frost Ring remains IsPvEArena and follows ArenaBattleService.
- Campaign champion display names and authenticated account names are separate
  identities; game-event delivery must route by the explicit network binding.
- Temporary bootstrap exposes eight independently persisted race champions. Every
  campaign request, deck/talent update, and engine initializer must use the
  selected champion's race, not Campaign:DefaultRace (except as legacy fallback).
- The Human test champion's ID/name and existing campaign JSON remain stable.
- The original HEX rules/AI path is preferred over custom battle/rules ports.
- Campaign battle results must be applied by the campaign layer after the final game
  session events have flushed, then delivered as ServiceCampaign state updates.

AZ1 entry protocol (first testable slice; live client validation pending):
    completed Crayburn QUEST Step2 + retained PANORAMA
      -> CaptainCedric/race IntroNpc authored travel conversation
      -> verified client event choice_go (or choice_stay)
      -> choice_go: atomic AREA creation/resume + CurrentCampaignId change
      -> normal PANORAMA response first
      -> FIFO unsolicited AREA cmpupdate (feralroot_travel, Transition=true)
      -> client fetches AREA summary (CampType 2, campaign/azmap/azmap,
         campaign/az01/nodes) and original client renders its map
      -> AREA begins at Node001 Into The Woods; Node002 initially visible.
    choice_stay does not create AREA. After the choice_go notification, the
    client may still send conv_done for the old PANORAMA; its response never
    reassigns CurrentCampaignId away from AREA. qcur4champ and profile
    LastCampaignID resolve AREA across restarts. Only opening map topology is
    currently modeled; no unverified Node002 dialogue/quest is seeded.
